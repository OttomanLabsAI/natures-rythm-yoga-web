Hello from Nature's Rhythm

Our studio, your studio

Nature's Rhythm is a lifestyle practice that has evolved naturally over 26 years with your lead teacher, Andrea — a practice that places importance on attuning to nature's cyclical rhythms.

The training

Becoming a teacher, as a natural unfolding

The training shapes the teacher you become — not only how you teach, but how you walk through the world.

For many people, becoming a yoga teacher is a threshold moment. This is an immersive training rooted in embodied practice and explored through satsang: as you deepen your practice and embody your yoga, teaching emerges naturally.

Rather than rushing the process in a three-to-four-week intensive, the course is designed as a seven-month immersion — one full day each week, allowing space for deeper integration and embodiment.

Alongside your weekly training you'll receive seven months of unlimited access to every class at Yoga in the Stars, and join a series of enrichment experiences with the wider collective of facilitators — including guest-list access to the Paradhis festival in Cornwall.

After graduation, you're warmly invited to remain part of the community as you find your footing as a teacher.

The rhythm of your training

Like the moon, the course moves through phases — each month building on the last.

April

Arrive & root

Meet your cohort, settle into the space, establish your daily practice.

May

Breath & asana

Foundations of pranayama and posture, explored through embodiment.

June

Philosophy & satsang

Yogic philosophy held in open discussion, in the spirit of community.

July

The art of teaching

Sequencing, voice and holding space — teaching practice begins.

August

Community immersion

Enrichment experiences with the wider collective, including Paradhis in Cornwall.

September

Integration & graduation

Consolidate, teach, and step out with your community behind you.
